# Malaria — HorizonCare Knowledge Entry

## Topic

Malaria

## Summary

Malaria is an infectious disease caused by Plasmodium parasites and transmitted mainly through the bites of infected female Anopheles mosquitoes.

Common symptoms can include fever, chills, headache, sweating, weakness, and other flu-like symptoms.

Symptoms alone do not establish a diagnosis. Appropriate testing and clinical assessment are required.
